# Broadcast production and delivery

## What should a professional episode look and sound like?

### Takeaway

Build an authored package that introduces a real development immediately, adds visual evidence as the anchor explains it, and uses a recognizable sting, type system, source treatment, and mix throughout. The primary format is an edited morning sports segment with the density and pacing of a news desk.

### Cited Findings

- The present renderer produces one 1920 × 1080 Canvas plate per chapter, uses `am_michael` by default, makes ASS caption timings from narrated parts and character weights, and merges the plates, captions, progress graphic, and voice in FFmpeg. Its validator checks dimensions, codec and duration. The code already has seams for narration injection, `ffprobe`, timeouts, an isolated render worker and chapter starts. [Current renderer](../../server/sports/produce.mjs), [Render worker](../../server/sports/render-worker.mjs), [FFprobe wrapper](../../server/media.mjs).
- The separate scripture path already uses Whisper to align a known transcript to the rendered narration. Abstract the generally useful alignment step and preserve the scripture-specific mapper. [Existing word alignment](../../server/align.mjs), [Shared voice module](../../server/voice.mjs).
- EBU R95 describes 5% graphics safe and 3.5% action safe per edge in 16:9. These are broadcast references, and YouTube UI overlays and captions need independent placement checks. [EBU R95](https://tech.ebu.ch/publications/r095/).
- The existing Atkinson Hyperlegible font and proposed Barlow Condensed display family both have OFL releases. Version/pin the actual downloaded font binaries, include license notices when distributing fonts, and test character coverage. [Atkinson OFL](https://github.com/google/fonts/blob/main/ofl/atkinsonhyperlegible/OFL.txt), [Barlow Condensed project metadata](https://github.com/google/fonts/blob/main/ofl/barlowcondensed/METADATA.pb).
- YouTube's first-30-seconds measure evaluates whether the opening holds viewer attention and matches the title/thumbnail. Studio typically takes one to two days to populate audience retention. Key-moment highlights may require at least 60 seconds runtime and 100 views. [YouTube Audience Retention](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9314415).

### Inferences

- **Editorial promise.** Start with a reported turn in the game or an attributed consequence within 5 seconds. Aim for 3–5 minutes and 2–3 substantive stories when evidence supports that runtime. A 90-second lead analysis is a valid first fixture and a short morning dispatch is a valid quiet-day format. Start each segment on the news, then show the turning point, explain the consequence, and add one concrete thing to watch. Give Isaac a distinctly labeled opinion segment and don't simulate banter or attribute synthetic performances to him. Invite an actual Isaac/host WAV take through the editor for signature episodes and as the preferred lead audio if available, with consent. Preserve Kokoro for reliably scheduled narration; use a pronunciation lexicon keyed by player ID with human-approved annotations and sample reads. Preserve reported spelling in SRT and graphics even when pronunciations require a separate spoken form.
- **House timeline for a 3:30 cut.** 00:00–00:08 cold open with the supported lead and a context-matching play or full-screen score graphic. 00:08–00:10 original title sting. 00:10–00:21 short anchor setup with the final score and game identifier. 00:21–01:23 lead breakdown, broken into correct game footage, one contextual metric, and a play sequence or tactical replay. 01:23–01:24 segment transition. 01:24–02:16 second story. 02:16–02:17 segment transition. 02:17–02:49 Isaac's take. 02:49–03:20 what changes next. 03:20–03:30 concise end card. Treat ranges as a beat sheet, vary by fresh evidence, and avoid narrating artificial silence just to meet a duration.
- **Visual package brief.** Original Overtime Desk brand with deep charcoal, warm-white key type, and the existing restrained lime accent. Use tabular scores, 12-column grid, outer safe rectangle x=96–1824 and y=54–1026 at 1080p, a neutral type hierarchy, and a single restrained scoreboard accent. House minimums to test at scaled playback are roughly 72 px display, 42 px text and stat labels, 30 px credits when practical. Open on an accurate single point, resolve within 1–2 lines per caption, and put essential statements and all graphic elements inside designed clear zones. Dedicated title design with motion cue sequence (field line angle, wordmark resolve, single sonic signature), logo bug, an opener and end card, 8–14-frame stinger limited to chapter changes, J/L cuts or normal cuts between supporting shots, subtle animation on meaningful data changes, and independent theme variants for league-neutral full-screen cards. Bench decorative chapter numbers and nonstop crawls. Use both shape/text and color for lead change, status, and outcome.
- **Design templates.** Scoreline with explicit status including Final or OT; quarter/period/inning scores; top/bottom with current state; clearly labeled comparison stat with period/scope/unit; event timeline with game clock and explicit elapsed-vs-remaining; original tactical diagram with documented events and visible reconstruction label; labeled Isaac's take; sport-specific text slate; full-bleed legal clip with reversible source/date overlays; side-by-side clip and explanatory graphic; source card and disclosure/credits; responsive lower thirds; thumbnail with one focal subject and a factual takeaway. Author an approved graphics gallery with text bounding box collision detection and source bindings. Stills and clip framing should maintain scorebug, credit, game ball, and focal player when visible/required. Prefer an incidental original graphic to an unrelated stock crowd shot.
- **Clips as editorial evidence.** Have staff identify the specific play in the same game, and log source time in/out separately from game clock, rights window, source credits, animation permissions, and asset license ID. Let a short approved natural-sound beat breathe at a meaningful moment. Show full event action before cutaway when the action itself proves a story claim. Freeze, telestrate, slow or zoom only within grant and mark Replay or Analysis when it could be confused as live. Never show a different game as the game in the narration. If exact footage can't clear, state “Play diagram” on an original reconstruction plus a corroborated scoreline, timeline, stat card or approved editorial photo. The narration, identity and mix should work in a footage-free mode. A rights-cleared local/provider-supplied clip suffices for the pilot and an actual file authorized for channel upload is still required for the real-clip acceptance gate.
- **Audio as three buses.** Use VO, music/SFX, and licensed play audio or ambience with independent faders and revisioned stems. Buy or commission one human-composed signature (2 sec sting, :06 and :15 variants, :30 or reusable instrumental bed, no-vocal alt and separated rhythm/melody stems as useful) and pick two music moods. Use licensed temporary music, or VO-only while final stems are pending. Leave beds out of consequential quotes, avoid constant hype and audio over explanation, and emit an audio cue sheet with start/end, source and gain. Add headroom, loudness and true peak checks to the final output, not just individual input samples. Start at final -16 LUFS integrated ±1 LU and ≤ -1 dBTP after AAC, -1.5 dBTP pre-encode target; during speech target music approximately 16–22 dB below dialogue and evaluate on phone speakers and headphones. Treat values as Overtime Desk house defaults. Use gentle dynamics/de-essing when recordings need them and short crossfades, not crossfades that clip words. One actual voice track, no personality imitation. Use the description's transparent narration credit and a reviewed per-episode YouTube synthetic-media flag as applicable. Keep realistic synthetic game footage and simulated broadcaster voices out of the format. [YouTube GenAI disclosure examples](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14328491?hl=en), [YouTube video insert metadata](https://developers.google.com/youtube/data/reference/rest/v3/videos/insert), [ITU BS.1770-5 measurement method](https://www.itu.int/rec/R-REC-BS.1770-5-202311-I), [EBU R128 program level is -23 LUFS for its broadcast workflow](https://tech.ebu.ch/publications/r128), [FFmpeg audio filters](https://ffmpeg.org/ffmpeg-filters.html).

### Gaps

- A graphics art director, sonic signature and real licensed game assets remain production decisions and delivery work. The timings are prototypes. Test for taste, headline/thumbnail coherence, comprehension and normalized 30-second retention against the pilot's own baseline when there are meaningful sample counts.

## How should the renderer and review desk produce the package reliably?

### Takeaway

Separate shot planning and asset decisions from rendering so a reviewed episode revision yields the same editorial timeline without needing a live browser, stock-site access or model calls during encode. Start with the existing FFmpeg/Canvas worker plus authored motion plates and benchmark it on deployment hardware.

### Cited Findings

- FFmpeg directly supports overlays, scaling/fps/format normalization, trim/concat, transitions, subtitles, side-chain compression, mixing, single or two-pass loudness normalization, and artifact checks including `blackdetect`, `freezedetect`, and `silencedetect`. FFprobe can emit machine-readable stream, format and packet metadata. [FFmpeg filters](https://ffmpeg.org/ffmpeg-filters.html), [FFprobe](https://ffmpeg.org/ffprobe.html).
- The current Compose service has a two-CPU constraint and 1,800 MB memory limit on a shared 4-core, 3.8 GB box; the Bible app shares the service. The current isolated sports worker has a 40-minute wall limit and FFmpeg has a 20-minute encoding timeout. [Compose limits](../../deploy/docker-compose.yml), [Sports desk worker lifecycle](../../server/sports/desk.mjs), [Current renderer](../../server/sports/produce.mjs).
- Blender supports background rendering and animation image sequences; After Effects chooses format and channel handling through the Render Queue output module. Either can bake reusable frame sequences with alpha outside the runtime image. Remotion can express React timelines and runtime renders if technical, legal, and performance criteria are met. Remotion's v5 terms landing page labels itself upcoming, links current v4 terms, and v5 automation licensing may become material, so check the version actually installed and the team eligibility at implementation time. The FFmpeg project also documents that distributing GPL-enabled builds with libx264 has different license obligations. [Blender CLI rendering](https://docs.blender.org/manual/en/latest/advanced/command_line/render.html), [Adobe Render Queue](https://helpx.adobe.com/after-effects/desktop/render-and-export/basics-of-rendering-and-exporting/basics-rendering-exporting.html), [Remotion current v4 terms](https://www.remotion.dev/docs/terms-4-0), [Remotion upcoming v5 terms](https://www.remotion.dev/docs/terms), [FFmpeg licensing](https://ffmpeg.org/legal.html).

### Inferences

- **Renderer seam.** Turn `produce.mjs` into phases `planEpisode`, `validateTimeline`, `renderPreview`, `renderMaster`, and `qcEpisode`. A Zod-validated immutable versioned plan can reference `editionId`, `revision`, `claimIds`, `formatVersion`, `graphicsPackVersion`, `assetManifestHash`, `fps`, `shots[{id,template,startFrame,frames,primaryAsset,sourceRange,overlays,claimIds,fallbackTemplate}]`, `audioCues[{startSample,endSample,stem,kind,automation}]` and `captionCues`. Preserve `renderEpisode` as facade and `desk.mjs` revision/worker semantics. Check plan spans cover the full timeline, clip bounds and grant match, overlay rectangles avoid captions and source graphics, score/stat values resolve from confirmed claim IDs, and no remote URLs or unversioned fonts are rendered. Use stable input and derived-media hashes. Preserve original files, transform proxies only with permission, normalize VFR/interlace/HDR carefully, keep 16:9 composition intentional, and do not crop or stretch the wide source to remove logos/credits. Preserve gameplay motion where appropriate; direct a consistent 29.97 or 30 fps house timeline for the first mostly graphics/voice pilot, benchmark native-rate 59.94/60 sport footage and optimize separately if that dominates. Compile caption timings from final VO/stem alignment. Byte-identical outputs need not be promised across native builds, but plan and timing should be repeatable with pinned runtime.
- **Template/tool division.** Canvas/Skia generates dynamic, font-metric-aware graphic layers. FFmpeg composes, mixes, normalizes and writes MP4. Bake original opener, wipe, lower-third reveal texture, and end-card motion in Blender or AE as reusable plates, ingest alpha PNG sequence source or a compact lossless alpha-capable mezzanine supported by the chosen worker. Run neither desktop authoring tool on the cron critical path. Use fixed keyframes rather than a generic uniform animation across stories. Prototype Remotion solely if layout/motion complexity justifies chromium and package overhead after benchmarking P95/peak RAM and license entitlement. Store design masters, palette, font notices, authored components and example data under versioned local asset manifests. Archive large plates and vendor media in private content-addressed storage. Share font tokens between desk preview and render, retain current Chrome-free player for a Tier 1 worker. Add a render-only explicit fixture CLI with local inputs, no upload and no fresh LLM.
- **Workload budget (initial target, benchmark before commitment).** Always process on rolling ingest where permitted, queue one job per worker, isolate render and cap per stage; parallelism only after measuring. On named 2-vCPU / 1,800 MB target, fixture of 90 seconds mixes an authorized clip or graphics fallback plus all core motion and audio; aim within 6 minutes cold and 3 minutes cached. Target candidate 3–5 minute pilot preview ≤3 minutes at proxy resolution, full 1080p final ≤15 minutes warm P95, worker memory comfortably under limit, and edit only one scene → proxy in ≤60 seconds by caching VO and unaffected shots. Record timings, peak RSS, frames and disk writes for 10 representative jobs; escalate heavy capability to a separate worker if show and Bible queues contend. Use periodic previews while selecting the initial art and creative package. Stage finished candidates early enough to measure and obtain verified processing latency before final cutoff. Maintain short cut capability if needed to meet daily SLA. Keep all render logs private.
- **First vertical slice.** Reproduce a historical real game from a locally licensed clip (one short supported play) and a frozen evidence packet. Produce one 90-second mini-episode with a cold open, 2-second sting, verified score/time and diagram, clean source credit, an Isaac take or signed-off stand-in, approved music sting and music bed, properly ducked mix, timed SRT, end card and thumbnail. Repeat with clip substituted by original diagram and verify it still feels like the same show. Repeat with music omitted and safe titles. Add a reviewed desktop+360 px contact sheet, headphones/speaker review, capture render time and CPU/RAM. Include deliberately long names, multiple accents, OTA caption safe-area test, silent VO/sound, expiry with same content already cached, oversized thumbnail, incompatible frame rate, missing media, and interrupted worker cases.
- **Hard QA gates.** Run fully offline render with both allowed and expired fixtures. Fully decode output and inspect `ffprobe` JSON for resolution, H.264 progressive SDR (BT.709), yuv420p, square pixels, AAC-LC 48 kHz stereo, nonzero duration, timestamp monotonicity and A/V alignment within 80 ms. Inspect mixed audio with BS.1770 measure after encode for house LUFS/true peak, flag unintended black >0.25 s, silence >2 s, and frozen video beyond planned still-card intervals. Use beat-aware QA so intentionally silent title or approved designed holds are excluded. Check SRT time order/no overlaps, in-range captions, 42 chars/line max, two lines, ~17 CPS target and 95th percentile cue edge within 150 ms of spoken onset on manually audited pilot, resolved names/numbers and licensed original speech where meaningful. Check card bounds with long-text fixtures and mobile safe layout with YouTube player CC. Editorial reviewer sees a dated preview with visuals linked to claim/play IDs, a side-by-side editor-vs-source score check, asset rights statuses, storyboard and revision-specific approval. Render pack should emit `episode.mp4`, clean/optional open-caption preview, `captions.en.srt`, `thumbnail.jpg`, `timeline.json`, `credits.json`, `cue-sheet.json`, and `qc.json`. Only delivery files are fed to the uploader. Stash higher quality mezzanine/stems only if needed in storage where rights permit them.

### Gaps

- Current 2-vCPU RAM sizing and performance of the substantially richer timeline have not been measured. Benchmark cold/warm and check `FFMPEG_PATH` build filters and source color conversions on deployed Docker/Linux and Windows. No basis for an external rendering budget or tool subscription until the first benchmark.

## How does an approved cut reliably go live at 8 a.m.?

### Takeaway

Shift upload/YouTube processing ahead of the 8 a.m. checkpoint and keep exactly one public cut. Stage privately, verify the canonical ID and its processing status, check revision and rights immediately before the go-live action, and fail closed when the publication checks disagree.

### Cited Findings

- The app currently starts uploading after the 8 a.m. cron check and records a Google resumable session before media transfer. Completion and publication state are coupled, and editing is currently locked once a session exists. That's enough for delivery at 8 but insufficient to ensure a ready public watch page at 8. [Desk publish flow](../../server/sports/desk.mjs), [Resumable YouTube integration](../../server/sports/youtube.mjs), [Cron job](../../deploy/sports.crontab).
- YouTube upload guidance favors MP4 fast start, H.264 progressive 4:2:0, 48 kHz AAC-LC and BT.709 SDR. Its content management guidance also favors preserving source frame rate where appropriate. [YouTube upload encoding](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1722171?hl=en-WS), [YouTube upload troubleshooting](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/58134?hl=en).
- `videos.insert` accepts `status.publishAt` for a private video with a future ISO 8601 timestamp. `videos.update` has owner write scopes beyond `youtube.upload`; it replaces mutable fields in the specified parts, so preserve reviewed status fields in updates. The `processingDetails` available via `videos.list` are accessible only to the owner. `processingDetails.processingStatus` provides a processing result and does not certify copyright/rights. A staged future publish timestamp in the past causes immediate public visibility. [Upload fields](https://developers.google.com/youtube/data/reference/rest/v3/videos/insert), [Update semantics and scopes](https://developers.google.com/youtube/data/reference/rest/v3/videos/update), [Video resource](https://developers.google.com/youtube/data/reference/rest/v3/videos), [Owner processing details](https://developers.google.com/youtube/data/reference/rest/v3/videos/list).
- `captions.insert` accepts timed tracks and requires `youtube.force-ssl` or `youtubepartner`; it is currently documented at 400 quota units. `sync` is unsupported per the YouTube revision history despite stale language on the method page. UTF-8 plain SubRip is supported. `thumbnails.set` supports the current `youtube.upload` scope and a JPG/PNG, while actual channel capabilities must be confirmed. Keep a reasonable house JPG cap (e.g. 2 MB for portable workflows), sample mobile crops and use 16:9. YouTube's manual chapter markup calls for starting at 00:00, at least three ascending timestamps and 10-second minimum chapters. [Captions method](https://developers.google.com/youtube/data/reference/rest/v3/captions/insert), [Revision history](https://developers.google.com/youtube/data/revision_history), [Supported caption files](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/2734698?hl=en), [Thumbnails method](https://developers.google.com/youtube/data/reference/rest/v3/thumbnails/set), [YouTube chapters](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9884579?hl=en).

### Inferences

- **Recommended production design.** Make 00:00 Central first rundown, overnight refreshes and provisional builds, editorial window with Isaac's approvals and hold control, cut revision lock based on measured rendering and YouTube processing time (start trial at 06:45–07:00), final render and private upload with captions and thumbnail, and a final revision/rights/processing/hold check immediately before 08:00 Central cron update on the same video ID. The public time objective is 8 a.m. ship decision, with platform propagation subject to YouTube. Store plan hash and video ID with the private upload. No broad permission or new quota assumption at prototype stage. Continue to use the current uploader until private-stage integration is tested with a channel owner who reconsents to required scopes. Optional ready-checked `publishAt` uses already-processed video and a locked revision if choosing platform scheduling, but weigh the loss of a last-second rights/hold veto. Do not pre-schedule two candidate IDs or leave a stale cut scheduled.
- **Idempotency and incidents.** New statuses `draft`, `rendered`, `checked`, `staged/processing`, `readyToAir`, `published`, `held` and `needsReconciliation` can use internal substates of present reducer. 07:58 correction invalidates approval and ready marker; answer with a fresh current candidate when time and policy permit, or hold and show Isaac why. Both app-initiated and owner-entered private videos must match channel/video ID/revision and current asset grant window. Recheck statuses after network ambiguity, never blind reupload; reconcile on canonical ID. Restrict public availability to the permitted territories where the channel has an actual supported operation, otherwise choose globally licensed material or fallback. Test release Dry Run with current scheduler DST tests and simulated restart, missing rights, quota, delayed processing, canceled scheduled cut, and late update during lock. Owner-controlled live smoke verifies Studio and public watch-page state after 08:00 with a real token once authorized. Explicitly track separate metrics for uploaded, processed and public, report out every miss and distinguish platform delay.

### Gaps

- Production OAuth scope, YouTube channel eligibility, quota, latency distribution, Content ID behavior, private archive and rights revocation policy need authorized real-channel validation. The normal Data API processing check is no substitute for clearing Content ID and consulting Studio or the licensed provider as agreed.
