# Real game clips, static assets, and music

## Which sources can supply actual game footage?

### Takeaway

Run two tracks. Prove the production flow with a written grant from one adult independent or regional league and its production partner, then negotiate a dated current-event package with a major league or an agency that can supply the specific events and publication rights. Keep a second, fully designed, graphics-led cut available for each story.

### Cited Findings

- **NFL.** NFL Films offers a public footage licensing request for people who are neither current nor former players. Use that intake and ask about latest-game highlights, cleared audio, delivery deadlines and the actual YouTube grant. Public information establishes an archive and request channel. It gives no daily feed, term, or download commitment. [NFL Films licensing](https://www.nflfilms.com/licensing/)
- **MLB.** MLB publishes its Footage Licensing contact, MLBfootage@mlb.com, for its archive and location shoots. Its March 2024 memo aimed at TV and radio outlets separately requires advance specific written permission for online use of highlights. Treat it as evidence of a narrow broadcast license, and confirm current terms and authorized digital counterparty directly. MLB's published Jomboy partnership illustrates bespoke negotiated access. [MLB footage and photos](https://www.mlb.com/official-information/about-mlb), [MLB immediate news memo, especially paragraphs 4 and 10](https://img.mlbstatic.com/mlb-images/image/upload/mlb/uewhf68z4yyazojfl34k.pdf), [MLB and Jomboy announcement](https://www.mlb.com/press-release/press-release-mlb-and-jomboy-media-to-forge-strategic-partnership)
- **NBA and NHL.** The NBA Media Central application is for regular coverage and subject to approval and site use restrictions. Start a league communications or licensing inquiry and obtain a deal for the actual digital use. NBA.com separately identifies controlled video, logos and statistics. The NHL accepts league-related inquiries and identifies ownership of league and club marks in its terms. Ask the league or the specific authorized broadcaster to identify the rights owner for each competition and distribution. [NBA Media Central terms and registration](https://mediacentral.nba.com/register/), [NBA Terms of Use](https://www.nba.com/termsofuse), [NHL league contact](https://www.nhl.com/info/contact-us), [NHL Terms of Service](https://www.nhl.com/info/terms-of-service)
- **AP and SNTV.** AP and IMG's SNTV supplies sports action and sports news video to digital and broadcast customers. AP Media API explicitly offers a searchable or continuous feed of licensed content, including video, pictures, graphics and audio, according to contract. Ask AP to identify rights by league, match, territory, highlight versus interview package, and deadlines. This is a strong enterprise candidate for multi-sport overnight coverage subject to a contract with suitable entitlements. [AP sports licensing](https://www.ap.org/content/topics/sports/), [SNTV at AP](https://www.ap.org/partners/sntv/), [AP Media API entitlements](https://api.ap.org/media/v/docs/Overview.htm)
- **Reuters, IMG and production vendors.** Reuters Connect lists sports images and video. Its FAQ limits single-asset video to archive footage over 30 days old, so discuss subscription if evaluating it for last night's action. IMG offers rights and archive services and publicly documents individual competitions it represents. WSC Sports advertises a workflow for rightsholders to create and distribute clips. Treat it as a possible delivery method via an authorized partner. [Reuters sports catalog](https://www.reutersconnect.com/collection/sports-coverage), [Reuters plan and permission FAQ](https://www.reutersconnect.com/plans-and-pricing), [IMG Rights](https://www.img.com/capabilities/rights), [IMG Bundesliga archive agreement](https://www.img.com/our-news/bundesliga-and-img-renew-international-video-archive-agreement), [WSC Sports workflow](https://lp.wsc-sports.com/hubfs/the%20ias%20playbook%20updated%20version%202025.pdf)
- **Local pilot.** Negotiate an actual clip delivery agreement with an adult independent league, event organizer, and/or local broadcaster that can substantiate its ability to license the camera feed and event rights. Obtain permission for separately captured local footage before filming. As an example of potential overlapping terms, NFHS has named a multimedia rights agent and individual state associations publish separate event media policies. A media viewing pass does not establish our show's permission to download or republish. [NFHS multimedia rights announcement](https://nfhs.org/stories/nfhsnfhs-network-signs-agreement-with-tpg-to-manage-sponsorship-and-multimedia-rights), [Kentucky high school media rights example](https://khsaa.org/common_documents/handbook/2024-2025/Supplemental/2024-25%20Policies-08-Media%20Relations-Video%20Rights%20and%20Sports%20Information%20Policies%20from%20Documents.pdf), [NFHS Network terms](https://www.nfhsnetwork.com/termsofuse)

### Inferences

- Send the producer a plain-language request for a four-week one-league pilot with 1–3 short play clips per covered game, regular overnight availability, a next-morning delivery SLA, a contract identifier and explicit usage policy per asset. Request the names of camera feed, producer, distributor, league/event rightsholder, photography partner, talent and audio rightsholders as relevant. Request clean-feed and clean-audio options, event IDs, game clock/timecode, signed downloads or secure push, and availability of revisions or takedown notices.
- Ask specifically for the named YouTube channel and edit/reproduction/streaming/VOD rights, monetization and sponsorship, archive term, granted territories (prefer worldwide for a standard channel), embargo, scheduled publication, time windows and duration caps, number of episodes, subtitles and alternate cuts, pause/freeze/replay/crop/annotations, thumbnail/trailer/Shorts uses, required credits and visible marks, derived copies and retention, video-only versus broadcast, announcer, crowd, or arena music as separate permissions, and Content ID prerelease/claim contacts. Ask about contract scope, quote/service caps and restrictions on vendor AI, analysis and export. If footage is video-only, remove original audio entirely and use authorized narration, music or silence.
- Commission original play diagrams for the fallback and label them “Play diagram” or “Illustration.” Use a real, licensed still from that game when available. Keep the narration valid on either recut. Never swap in footage from another matchup as if it depicted the play.

### Gaps

- No provider has offered terms to Overtime Desk. Exact daily league coverage, speed, costs, logo clearance, territorial availability, audio and Content ID handling remain contract-specific. No general NBA/NHL creator raw-footage API was verified.

## How should static assets and footage enter the product?

### Takeaway

Separate media discovery from clearance, then bind each shot to the approved asset, contract version and exact clip timecodes. Auto-ingest only a signed provider integration or an authorized local drop with a producer-reviewed rights record.

### Cited Findings

- Getty's standard US agreement restricts commercial, promotional and other uses of editorial material unless explicitly authorized, limits modifications, and restricts machine learning and AI use of content including captions and metadata, subject to narrow creative-asset exceptions. Obtain explicit rights for monetized YouTube editorial use, cuts, branding treatments, thumbnails and automated analysis if desired. Getty credits and separate restrictions are asset-specific. [Getty Images US content license](https://www.gettyimages.com/eula), [Getty rights and clearance](https://www.gettyimages.com/rights-and-clearance)
- MLB Photos supplies official stock baseball photography by inquiry. AP and Reuters license static sports photography as distinct products. Natural Earth places its map data in the public domain. For font files, retain font family version, copyright, full license and distribution notices. [MLB Photos](https://www.mlb.com/official-information/about-mlb), [AP sports licensing](https://www.ap.org/content/topics/sports/), [Reuters sports coverage](https://www.reutersconnect.com/collection/sports-coverage), [Natural Earth terms](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/), [SIL Open Font License](https://openfontlicense.org/)
- U.S. Copyright Office distinguishes facts from protected expression, and its fair-use guidance applies four factors to each case. YouTube developer policies restrict downloading, importing, caching or storing YouTube audiovisual content without prior written YouTube approval. A stats/data feed, public image CDN, visible highlight, media credential or embed should enter the system only in its authorized role. [Copyright Office on facts and expression](https://www.copyright.gov/help/faq/faq-protect.html), [Copyright Office fair use factors](https://www.copyright.gov/fair-use/more-info.html), [YouTube API developer policies](https://developers.google.com/youtube/terms/developer-policies)
- YouTube's regional blocking guidance describes rights-management features in Studio Content Manager, while the normal Data API exposes region restriction information and does not list it among editable video properties for uploads. Plan worldwide grants for ordinary uploads unless a partner has explicit tested enforcement for all required territories. [YouTube territory controls](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/6303378), [YouTube videos resource](https://developers.google.com/youtube/data/reference/rest/v3/videos), [YouTube videos.update](https://developers.google.com/youtube/data/reference/rest/v3/videos/update)

### Inferences

- Use a private artifact store with separate raw masters, compatible proxies and renders. A catalog result starts as metadata-only. The delivery adapter maps `providerAssetId`, event ID, captured time, caption/credit, territory, permitted uses, ingestion route and title to a verified `rightsGrantId`. Keep immutable contract revisions and audit records separate from ordinary app responses. Explicit fields for video, still, announcer, crowd, music, logos and people, embargo, term, archive policy, publishing targets, derivative permissions, clip cap, credit, and claim contact. A content digest identifies the bytes and the shot binds `sourceInMs/sourceOutMs` and a specific grant version to the edit. Legal territory intersection is over all rendered shots, images and cues. Enforce deadlines separately for acquisition, publish, public availability and retention.
- Ingest authenticated, time-limited files from explicit adapter destinations. Do not treat story links or model suggestions as media download URLs. Validate file type, dimensions, duration, codec and checksum, and quarantine problems. Use allowlisted hosts and storage paths, enforce size and time limits, retry with backoff, normalize into high-quality mezzanine, generate lightweight internal contact sheets and thumbnails only where allowed. Distinguish CPU media analysis from multimodal, third-party or model processing and grant the latter only if the contract does. Human producer picks precise timecodes if automated analysis is unlicensed.
- Cache by provider asset/version and content hash under provider-specific retention; do not serve private source media to the public. For each use, run `canUse(asset, grant, surface, outputTerritories, plannedAirAt)` before inclusion, private preview or private YouTube upload (if permitted), scheduling and at the publish check. Verify derivative/tool permissions too. A revocation or expired license invalidates unposted manifests and follows the agreed published-episode response (replace where possible, otherwise unlist/private/remove according to contract and workflow). Provide an audit log and producer controls for “clear,” “awaiting rights,” “restricted,” “embargoed,” and “revoked.”
- Ranking policy: exact-event verified cleared gameplay, then an exact-event cleared still or presser, then an original data graphic, diagram or host shot. Treat licensed generic texture/venue exterior as atmosphere and label when context could mislead. Use original type, custom geometric court/field diagrams and text team abbreviations until marks and brand art are cleared. Enforce genuine official score data separately, never rasterize another publisher's scoreboard or chart as the substitute.
- Test fixtures cover late embargo, expired grant, video-only grant plus received mixed audio, static-only rights used in a teaser, geography mismatch, unauthorized source host, revoked asset on a previous render, Content ID claim, and matched event IDs across tabs. Supply two versions of the golden pilot, one with cleared real game clips and one with completely approved original graphics. Teams may approve one pilot's assets while procurement handles more.

### Gaps

- No private feed contract or rights counsel is supplied. Keep contract counsel or rights producer responsible for written permission and edge-case use decisions. Inventory current repo fonts and art to verify actual files and licenses before a distribution build.

## How can the show get a reliable music package and publish it?

### Takeaway

Commission a sonic identity with broad written rights for repeated use, and curate a small approved library of story beds. Build a silent or owned-music fallback into every rundown. Treat YouTube checks as operational signals and licensing as a separate verification.

### Cited Findings

- YouTube Studio Audio Library offers royalty-free music and SFX. Tracks tagged Creative Commons require attribution. YouTube says copyright-safe Audio Library downloads are free of rightsholder Content ID claims and eligible for YPP monetization. Confirm license per track and scope for any additional platform. Creator Music can offer track-specific licensing or revenue share. Most licenses beyond Audio Library are single-video/single-use for YouTube, unsuitable as the recurring sound of this daily show without separate rights. [YouTube Audio Library](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/3376882), [Creator Music usage details](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/11611019)
- Epidemic Sound and Artlist document account, plan and channel coverage. Artlist explains clearlisting and retention of published project rights subject to plan terms and active publishing. These are options to vet based on the owning entity, YouTube channel, publishing/retention date and usage, with written confirmation for the intended business/team use where needed. [Epidemic Sound licensing](https://www.epidemicsound.com/pricing-and-plans/), [Artlist license](https://help.artlist.io/hc/en-us/articles/29490991524253-Understanding-Artlist-s-license), [Artlist Clearlist](https://help.artlist.io/hc/en-us/articles/29648622402333-Understanding-Artlist-s-Clearlist)
- The Copyright Office treats a composition and its recorded performance as separate works. Commissioning has specific U.S. work-made-for-hire requirements. Get an executed assignment or license covering both rights and all contributors, with sample clearances and explicit rights for repeating stems, cutting and mixing. [U.S. Copyright Office Sound Recordings](https://www.copyright.gov/engage/docs/recording.pdf), [U.S. Copyright Office Circular 30](https://copyright.gov/circs/circ30.pdf)
- YouTube distinguishes reused-content review from copyright. Its monetization policies give analysis of sports replays as an example of meaningful contribution while warning against interchangeable mass-produced content. Shared branding can recur. A passing channel review or transformative editorial contribution does not confer media rights. YouTube says automated Checks are not final, and a short use can still attract claims. The Content ID API is limited to YouTube content partners. Rightsholder allowlisting applies to future matching by the partner and does not automatically clear existing claims. Multiple controlling partners may require separate allowlisting. [YPP channel monetization policy](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392), [YouTube fair use help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9783148), [YouTube upload Checks](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/57407), [Content ID API eligibility](https://developers.google.com/youtube/partner), [Content ID allowlisting](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/6070344)
- Private unbroadcast videos can be scheduled through `status.publishAt` on a YouTube video resource. YouTube's GenAI help describes disclosure expectations and the videos API includes `status.containsSyntheticMedia`. Check current instructions at release, especially if using AI-generated music or realistic altered footage. [YouTube videos resource](https://developers.google.com/youtube/data/reference/rest/v3/videos), [YouTube GenAI disclosure](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14328491)

### Inferences

- Commission a short Overtime Desk sting, 6–8 second open variant, 20–40 second editable underscore, separate risers/impacts, 2–3 second transition cues and closer using the same approved motif. Get clean 48 kHz WAV stems and an edits folder containing no unlicensed samples. Keep the recurring branding in commissioned or demonstrably reusable content. Choose instrumental beds by pacing and story tone, duck music under narration and leave space for a rights-cleared natural sound moment. Use authored graphics and licensed track credits; no theme lift from an existing network.
- Log track/version, composer/performer/ISRC where available, provider, rights proof, channel clearlist state, attribution text, episode and exact cue timecodes. Generate a cue sheet and required video description text from the same shot/cue manifest. If subscription lapses or terms change, existing and future episodes follow their own terms, not the assumption that a downloaded file stays reusable.
- Allowlist before launch where the counterpart can actually do so, and obtain a same-day human claim escalation contact. Private preflight only under grants that allow upload to YouTube. Pin and preflight the exact asset/encoding hash and do not treat processing complete as claim clearance. For initial pilots a producer can look at YouTube Studio's checks. Hold the licensed cut or schedule a separately produced cleared version if unresolved. Keep claim evidence and actions under human control; no automated claim disputes. For the 8 a.m. deadline schedule a private upload ahead of air with a clear late-edit cutoff, or publish the checked render through a release command at 8 a.m. if licensing requires remaining private through an embargo.

### Gaps

- No durable audio deal or YouTube channel details supplied. The app should use a local cleared fixture or silence until a producer enrolls the actual channel and certifies track terms.
